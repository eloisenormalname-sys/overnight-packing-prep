Keep your routine safe

Progress and edits are saved in this browser, on this device. They don’t sync to Google Sheets or other devices. Clearing website data can erase them. Export a backup to keep a copy or move to another device.

Future changes

Use Edit routine for personal changes. For app updates, give ChatGPT the GitHub repository and ask it to preserve item IDs and your saved progress. If you’ve edited an item locally, that edit takes priority over future default changes.

Setup & maintenance guide

Restoring the routine removes local edits; matching completion states are kept. Start fresh clears checkmarks only.
